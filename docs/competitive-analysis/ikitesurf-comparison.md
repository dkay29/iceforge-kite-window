# IceForge Kite Window vs iKitesurf

## Executive Summary

iKitesurf is an excellent weather information application. IceForge Kite
Window is intended to be a **decision-support system** that synthesizes
multiple environmental data sources and personal preferences into a
single recommendation.

Rather than replacing iKitesurf, IceForge should become the app users
open first to answer a single question:

> **"Should I go kitesurfing today, and if so, when?"**

## IceForge Differentiators

### Personalized Session Suitability Score

Produce a single ride-quality score (0--100%) based on:

- Wind speed
- Gustiness
- Wind direction
- Tide
- Current
- Wave height
- Air temperature
- Water temperature
- Weather
- Rain
- Sunrise/sunset
- User experience
- Preferred riding conditions

Example:

> **Today's conditions are a 92% match for your preferences.**

---

### Personalized Decision Engine

Different users receive different recommendations for identical
conditions.

Example:

- Beginner → Not recommended
- Intermediate → Good after 2:00 PM
- Advanced foiler → Excellent

---

### Timeline Visualization

Instead of tables of numbers, present an intuitive graph showing:

- Acceptable riding window
- Ride quality trend
- Best launch time
- Best pack-up time
- Sunrise
- Sunset

This becomes the primary application screen.

---

### AI Explanations

Instead of simply displaying weather data, explain the reasoning.

Example:

> Excellent three-hour session from 2:30--5:30 PM. Wind remains steady
> while the outgoing tide reduces shore break. Conditions deteriorate
> rapidly after 6 PM as the wind veers offshore.

---

### Trend Awareness

Recognize conditions such as:

- Wind building
- Wind fading
- Thermal filling
- Frontal passage
- Oscillating winds

The trend is often more valuable than the current reading.

---

### Learning User Preferences

After every session, collect feedback:

- Great session
- Underpowered
- Overpowered
- Too gusty
- Dangerous

Continuously refine recommendations.

## Product Positioning

IceForge should not compete directly with iKitesurf on raw weather
presentation.

Instead:

1.  Answer the go/no-go decision.
2.  Recommend the best session window.
3.  Explain the reasoning.
4.  Allow users to drill into detailed weather if desired.

## Vision

Move from a weather application to an AI-powered decision assistant.

**Weather apps answer:**

> What is the weather?

**IceForge answers:**

> Should I go kitesurfing today, when should I leave home, and why?
